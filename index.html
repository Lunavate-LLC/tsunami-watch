The verdict

Each of the 41 bellwether races is a tsunami sign, a wave sign, or short of the wave:

  • Toss-ups and leans (Maine, Texas, Virginia's 2nd): a Democratic win is a wave sign. Winning by 6 or more past the final poll is a tsunami sign. A loss is short.
  • Trump seats (Kentucky's 6th, South Carolina, Florida): a Democrat within 5 is a wave sign. A Democratic win is a tsunami sign.
  • The verdict: if half or more are tsunami signs, it's tsunami pace. If half or more are wave or tsunami signs, it's wave pace. Anything less is short of the wave.

    How the lines are set

    Toss-ups and leans: the wave line is a Democratic win. The tsunami line is 6 points past the final poll or the analysts' marker, about the size of Marquette's biggest final-poll miss since 2016. That line is our own working line, not a forecaster's.

    Seats Trump carried comfortably, and Republican-leaning statewide races: a Democrat within 5 is a wave sign and a Democratic win is a tsunami sign, following the early-race guides from NPR and Daily Kos.
